Delete your MolDock account

MolDock (Protein-Ligand Dock) is developed by John Seong. You can delete your MolDock account and the data stored with it at any time.

In the app

  1. Open MolDock and tap Account (the person icon on the home screen).
  2. Scroll to Delete account and tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm. The account is deleted straight away.

By email

If you can't use the app, email john@orchestrsim.com from the address on your account with the subject "Delete my MolDock account". We delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your email address and account record
  • Sign-in sessions and pending sign-in codes
  • REST API keys
  • Docking jobs and screening batches saved to your account
  • Lab quote requests sent from your email

Jobs submitted without an account keep their results but lose any notification email. Nothing is kept in backups.

Deleting your account does not cancel a MolDock Pro subscription bought through the App Store or Google Play. Cancel it in your store account so it doesn't renew.
